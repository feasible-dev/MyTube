About MyTube

Your description goes to OpenAI using your granted ChatGPT access and can use your plan allowance. ChatGPT searches the web for YouTube videos; MyTube checks sourced links and public titles with YouTube. No Google sign-in is needed for this search.

Matching cached videos appear first. New results follow the web search. An unusable response is retried once automatically; Your request shows the retry. Durations are added when existing YouTube authorization allows a metadata lookup, without asking you to sign in to Google. Upload date, views and spoken language are not verified through web search.

MyTube keeps interests, preferences, saved/hidden choices and public video metadata on this device. Video metadata expires after 30 days. Search requests remain until you clear them. Clear or disable it in Settings. Original search requests are saved locally when history is enabled. ChatGPT tokens are not saved in browser storage. This version does not refresh the home automatically.

Subscriptions are read only when you connect YouTube in the Subscriptions view. MyTube does not read viewing history, playback progress or browser cookies. Thumbnails load from YouTube; video links open YouTube normally. Existing Google access from earlier versions can be managed below.

Live results exclude explicit AI or synthetic-production disclosures detected in titles and descriptions. This limited English/French text heuristic does not inspect audio or video, authenticate channels, or detect undisclosed AI generation. Low view counts and AI-related topics alone are not exclusion criteria.

The initial NASA collection is a saved experiment, separate from new ChatGPT searches. Cached metadata expires after 30 days. This development preview has not had an independent security audit and is not approved for public distribution.
